## Selected quantitative research

### [Financial ML Validation](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/financial-ml-validation)

A frozen CatBoost classifier underperforms a simple probability baseline in a historical Treasury confirmation window. The study makes the information clock, purging and dependent uncertainty inspectable. [Treasury results](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/financial-ml-validation/blob/main/reports/real/REPORT.md)

### [Derivatives Pricing Lab](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/derivatives-pricing-lab)

European prices and Greeks are checked against QuantLib on 1,296 fixed cases. Separate studies cover simulated hedging costs and settlement-aware quote diagnostics. [QuantLib comparison](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/derivatives-pricing-lab/blob/main/reports/quantlib/REPORT.md)

### [Market Making Lab](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/market-making-lab)

In a shared-path simulation, allowing customers to walk away changes the ranking of quote policies. The study tracks fills, inventory, adverse selection and tail loss. [Demand experiment](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/market-making-lab/blob/main/reports/demand/REPORT.md)
